## What is AquaRide AI?

AquaRide AI is a cyber-physical automated washing bay for two-wheelers that uses **Edge Computer Vision**, **Differential Pressure Zoning**, and **Closed-Loop Water Recycling** to deliver a complete, safe, and eco-friendly wash in under 3 minutes using only 25 Liters of water - compared to 90 Liters in traditional manual washing.

## Key Features
- **AI Camera Dirt Detection** (YOLOv8 Edge TPU) - Real-time optical density mapping
- **Sensitive Component Shielding** (<35 Bar on ECU, TFT meters, switchgear)
- **4 Wash Modes**: Eco, Standard, Heavy Mud, AI Auto-Sense (dynamic)
- **MSME ROI Calculator** - Monthly P&L, payback period, water savings
- **IoT Fleet Telemetry Dashboard** - Bay-level tank levels, turbidity monitoring
- **Customer Mobile App Mockup** - QR booking, live CCTV, eco-receipt
- **Interactive Patent Dossier** - Form 2 IPO-ready claims with hardware pinouts
- **Hackathon Pitch Deck** - 10 slides + Judge Q&A Defense Engine

## Market Impact
- 260 Million+ two-wheelers in India
- 75% reduction in water usage (90L to 25L per wash)
- 16-month capex payback for MSME garage owners
